Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

1. Who we are

MENEW Digital Menus ("MENEW", "we") is a digital menu platform operated from Egypt. When you scan a QR code at a venue that uses MENEW, you interact with our service. Questions about this policy can be directed to omar@oezzelarab.com.

2. What we collect and why

Device identifier (essential)

We generate a random device ID and store it in a cookie and your browser's local storage, with a 7-day sliding expiry. This ID lets the menu remember your basket between page loads and recognise returning visits. We do not use fingerprinting techniques (no canvas, WebGL, font, or hardware enumeration).

Anonymous browsing events (analytics)

We record which items you view, how long they are visible, which videos you play, and what you add to or remove from your basket. These events are linked to your device ID, not your name or phone number. Venue owners see only aggregated statistics — they cannot identify individual visitors. You can decline this in the consent banner.

Phone number (only if you sign in)

If you choose to sign in with your phone number, we verify it via a one-time code sent over WhatsApp. Your number is never stored in plain text — it is encrypted with a key held only on our servers, and we store a separate one-way hash used solely to look you up. We display only a masked version (e.g. +20 ••• •••• 4567). We use your number to deliver the one-time code and, if you allow it, to notify you about your first-visit discount status. We never share it with venue owners or third-party marketers.

Preferences

If you set dietary or taste preferences (e.g. vegetarian, spicy), we store them on your profile to personalise the menu. You can change or clear them at any time.

3. How we share data

  • Venue owners receive aggregated analytics for their own venue. They never see individual device IDs or phone numbers.
  • Service providers — our database (Supabase/PostgreSQL) and hosting infrastructure process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations. One-time codes are delivered via the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.).
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to any third party.

4. How long we keep data

  • Anonymous analytics events are kept to maintain the accuracy of venue rankings and personalisation. They contain no name or contact information.
  • Your diner profile (phone hash, preferences, visit history) is kept until you delete your account. Deleting removes your profile; existing analytics rows are anonymised.
  • Redemption records for welcome discounts are kept for fraud-prevention and accounting purposes even after account deletion, in pseudonymised form.

5. Your rights

  • Decline analytics — tap "Decline" on the consent banner at any time.
  • Delete your account — tap "Delete my account" in your profile. Your phone entry and preferences are removed immediately.
  • Data access or questions — email omar@oezzelarab.com and we will respond within 14 days.
